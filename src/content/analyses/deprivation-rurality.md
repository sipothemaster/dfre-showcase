Unadjusted summaries show a strong difference in the breadth of the delivery market: the median fast-food count is 156 in the most deprived IMD decile and 82 in the least deprived. A typical rural LSOA has 9 deliverable fast-food restaurants at its representative postcode, compared with 123 in an urban LSOA.

Adjusted models use continuous IMD score as the main deprivation specification and account for rurality, population density and region. They retain uncertainty and show that the direction and shape of association differs across count, density and share.

The result is best read as spatial patterning in a digital market. Demand, physical restaurant location, platform operations, delivery logistics and other shared causes remain plausible explanations.
