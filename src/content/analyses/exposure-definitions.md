The project retains three exposure definitions before looking at outcomes. **Count** measures breadth of choice. **Density** standardises that count by resident population. **Share** asks what proportion of the available food market is classified as fast food.

These measures are correlated, but not interchangeable. Rural–urban contrasts are large for count and density while median share is much closer. At MSOA level, the analysis also distinguishes an opportunity-weighted share from the resident-weighted mean of LSOA shares.

This design prevents the preferred metric from being selected only because it produces a stronger headline or model result.
